What is an ETF?

An exchange-traded fund (ETF) is a basket of securities (stocks, bonds, commodities) that trades on an exchange like a single stock. One purchase gives instant diversification, usually at a low fee. How this page stays current

Prices and daily changes load live from CNBC market data every time you open the page (auto-refresh every 60 seconds while open). Five-year history files used on the Compare and Forecast pages are rebuilt every weekday after the US close by an automated job from Nasdaq market data. No manual updates, no stale tables.
