How the 3 paths are built

From the ETF's own last ~5 years of daily prices we measure: μ = average monthly (log) return, σ = monthly volatility, plus CAGR, max drawdown and trend (price vs 200-day average).

  • Base compounds the historical monthly drift μ for 12 months.
  • Bull compounds μ + ½σ (a sustained one-sided surprise).
  • Bear compounds μ − ½σ.

The band between bear and bull covers roughly ±½σ/month of surprise — wide for volatile funds, narrow for T-bill funds. This is descriptive statistics, not a prediction: markets are driven by future news (rates, earnings, geopolitics — see Today's News), which history cannot know.
